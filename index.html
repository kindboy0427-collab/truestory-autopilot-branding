TRUESTORY · OWNER OPERATED

Documented stories.
Independent production.

TrueStory_Autopilot is a private tool used by its operator to research documented events, assemble English-captioned short videos from archival photographs, and manage the operator’s own YouTube and Instagram channels.

This website provides app information and privacy disclosures. It is not a public sign-in service. Channel administration is restricted to the operator.

Google integration

With the operator’s permission, the tool uploads and schedules videos on the connected YouTube channel, reads channel and video information, and retrieves YouTube Analytics to evaluate the operator’s content and guide future production.

No viewer Google sign-in is offered on this website. Google access is authorized through Google’s consent screen, not by entering a password here.
