TRUESTORY_AUTOPILOT

Privacy Policy

Updated November 2, 2026. This disclosure covers the operator’s private TrueStory_Autopilot workflow and this informational website.

Google data accessed

The tool requests YouTube upload access, read-only YouTube account access, and read-only YouTube Analytics access. It accesses the connected channel’s identifier and metadata, video identifiers, upload and scheduling results, and authorized performance metrics such as views, engaged views, watch time, likes, comments, shares and subscriber changes. It does not request Gmail, Drive or contact access.

How the data is used

Google data is used to upload and schedule the operator’s content, check processing and publication, prevent duplicate uploads, monitor errors, and compare video performance. Performance summaries and editorial preferences may inform AI-assisted content planning where that workflow is enabled. This is strategy selection, not a claim that an AI model is trained on private channel data.

Storage and access

OAuth credentials and operational records are stored in the operator’s project environment. Credentials are not published on this informational website. Local dashboards and operational records are intended for the operator. No visitor account or sign-in is provided here.

Sharing

Google and Meta receive the material and requests needed to operate their respective channel features. Configured AI providers may process supplied editorial material and performance-derived planning guidance when enabled. OAuth credentials are not part of the intended AI planning input. Google data is not sold or used for advertising profiles. The website host may process ordinary request information according to its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

The project keeps operational records and content history for scheduling, duplicate prevention, recovery and performance comparison. Retention depends on the operator’s configured archive and refresh policies; immediate automatic deletion is not promised. Contact the operator to request deletion of stored Google data. Disconnecting access prevents future authorized API access but does not itself erase local operational records.

Revoke access

The operator can revoke the app in Google Account connections. Contact kindboy0427@gmail.com for data questions or deletion requests.

Google API disclosure

TrueStory_Autopilot’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
